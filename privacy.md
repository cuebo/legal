# cuebo 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-11-10

## 简体中文

cuebo 是可配置模型、能力、插件和 Skill 的个人 AI 助理。使用某项功能时，相关数据可能由设备发送给你选择的服务。请在启用能力和添加第三方内容前阅读以下说明。

### 保存的数据

对话、Agent 配置、记忆、插件和 Skill 等工作区内容保存在设备上。模型及其他服务的密钥保存在 iOS 钥匙串中。启用 iCloud 备份或同步后，所选工作区内容会进入你的 Apple iCloud 私有数据库；其存储和同步受 Apple 的条款及隐私政策约束。关闭同步不会自动删除 Apple 已保存的副本。

### 发送给模型和第三方服务的数据

发起对话时，应用会将当前对话所需的文字、你提供的附件，以及按配置启用的 Agent 指令、记忆和工具结果发送给你选择的模型服务商。模型服务商会收到调用所需的 API Key。模型服务商如何处理这些数据取决于其政策。你配置的 SSH、沙箱及其他外部服务也会收到执行相应操作所需的数据和凭据。

应用可从 GitHub 下载你选择的 Skill、插件和界面组件。你安装的插件在启用并获得相应能力后，可访问被授权的系统能力或网络域名；插件及目标网站如何处理数据取决于其代码和政策。请只安装可信来源的内容。

### 设备权限与浏览器

日历、家庭设备、位置、照片、麦克风和语音识别等能力只在你启用或请求相应功能并授予系统权限后使用。相关结果在你要求 Agent 处理时可能进入发送给模型的对话内容。内置浏览器会在设备上保存网站数据（包括登录状态）；访问的网站可能按各自政策收集信息。你可以在 iOS 设置中撤销系统权限。

### 保留和控制

你可以在应用中删除会话、Agent、插件等内容，或关闭相关能力及 iCloud 同步。第三方模型、网站、服务及 Apple 已接收的数据应按其各自政策管理。若需询问数据处理或请求协助，请联系 [codeloverql@gmail.com](mailto:codeloverql@gmail.com)。

## English

cuebo is a personal AI assistant with configurable models, capabilities, plugins, and Skills. Data relevant to a feature may be sent from your device to the service you choose when you use that feature.

### Stored data

Conversations, Agent settings, memory, plugins, Skills, and other workspace content are stored on your device. API keys and other service credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. If you enable iCloud backup or sync, selected workspace content is stored in your private Apple iCloud database, subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy. Turning sync off does not automatically erase copies already stored by Apple.

### Model and external services

When you start a conversation, the app sends the text needed for that conversation, attachments you provide, and enabled Agent instructions, memory, and tool results to the model provider you select. The provider receives the API key needed for the request. Its handling of that information is governed by its own policy. SSH, sandbox, and other external services you configure receive the data and credentials needed to carry out the requested action.

The app can download Skills, plugins, and UI components you select from GitHub. An enabled plugin may use authorized device capabilities or network domains. Data handling by plugins and destination sites depends on their code and policies; install content only from sources you trust.

### Device permissions and browser

Calendar, Home, location, photo, microphone, and speech recognition features are used only after you enable or request them and grant the relevant system permission. Results may become part of a model request when you ask the Agent to use them. The built-in browser stores website data, including sign-in state, on your device. Websites may collect information under their own policies. You can revoke device permissions in iOS Settings.

### Retention and choices

You can delete conversations, Agents, plugins, and other content in the app, or disable capabilities and iCloud sync. Manage information already received by Apple, model providers, websites, or other services under their respective policies.
